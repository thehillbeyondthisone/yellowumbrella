People, pets & movement

The capture works best when nothing moves. People and pets stay out of the room being captured; work can carry on elsewhere.

No-capture areas

List every room, closet, cabinet, floor, doorway, outdoor area, or object that has to be left out. Put them in your Capture Order, and if you can, mark the doors before we arrive.

People who will appear in the scene on purpose may need their own release. We generally recommend capturing spaces empty.
