Built for marketing, not for measuring.

These are faithful reconstructions of your plans, not measured drawings. Layouts follow your source drawings and are scaled to your advertised square footage. Anything the drawing doesn't show, like ceiling height or furniture size, is our estimate, and every viewer says so.
