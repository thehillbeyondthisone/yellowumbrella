What comes with every layout

One interactive viewer for the whole property, plus still images for everywhere a viewer can't go.

  • A 3D residence

    Turn it, zoom in, and focus on one room at a time. Show it furnished or empty, with cutaway or full-height walls, in warm evening light or daylight.

  • A clean plan view

    A top-down plan with room labels, drawn in the same finishes, so the layout reads at a glance.

  • Compare with the original

    Put the 3D version next to your brochure drawing, so nobody has to wonder whether it matches.

  • Walk inside

    A first-person walkthrough with a guided tour from the front door through every room. Add it to the layouts you want to feature.

  • Images for listings and print

    A hero image and a labeled plan for every layout at 2400 × 1800, ready for listing sites, flyers, and your price sheet. Save more from any angle.

  • Works wherever renters are

    Phones, tablets, and computers. Share it as a link, put it on your own website, or take an offline copy to a leasing appointment.

How it works

  1. 1

    Send your plans

    A brochure, price list, PDF, or CAD file, plus a few photos of your finishes if you have them.

  2. 2

    We build each layout

    Walls, doors, windows, fixtures, and furniture traced from your drawings and scaled to your square footage.

  3. 3

    You check it

    Review every layout next to the original and mark anything that should change before it goes live.

  4. 4

    It goes live

    You get a link, a version for your website, an offline copy, and the image set.

i

Built for marketing, not for measuring.

These are faithful visualizations of your plans, not measured drawings. Layouts follow your source drawings and are scaled to your advertised square footage. Anything the drawing doesn't show, like ceiling height or furniture size, is our estimate, and every viewer says so.

Need the real thing, as it stands today? Our 3D spatial capture records the actual rooms on site.
