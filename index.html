How a project goes

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea

    A sketch, a reference photo, or one sentence is enough to start. We'll ask about budget, timing, and where it's headed.

  2. 2

    See a prototype

    You get a model to spin, a build to play, or a proof to mark up before anything is final.

  3. 3

    We make it

    Code, models, and prints produced in-house, with check-ins at each milestone.

  4. 4

    Deliver it

    Printed goods get shipped. Games launch, and 3D scans arrive as a link you can share with anyone.
