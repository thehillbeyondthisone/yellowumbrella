Walk in at full size.

The same sample scenes from the demos page, in a headset instead of a window. You stand in the room at its real scale and walk around with the thumbsticks. It runs in the headset’s own browser — no app, no store, no account.

What you need

Anything that speaks WebXR. You are not installing anything.

  • A headset with a browserMeta Quest 2, 3, or Pro through the Meta Horizon browser; Apple Vision Pro through Safari; Pico and other WebXR headsets through their own browsers.
  • A decent connectionThe scene streams in while you look around, pulling only the detail your view needs. Wi-Fi is much happier than a phone hotspot.
  • Room to turn aroundYou can stay seated and use the thumbsticks, but a clear couple of metres makes it much better. Left stick moves, right stick turns.
  • Nothing elseNo app, no store page, no account, no download to manage. It is a link, and it opens in the browser that came with the headset.
